Architecture: Linux::Event::HTTP is a thin native Linux transport/adapter
around event-loop-independent Unblock::HTTP. Real Unblock Transactions
and Uniform Request/Response objects form the public boundary. No old
Request/Response/Transaction/Operation wrappers or legacy parser remains.
HTTP/1, TLS HTTP/2, combined TCP+QUIC/TLS HTTP/3, WebSocket and generic
stream-local Extended CONNECT are implemented. H3-only and wildcard UDP
listeners remain unsupported.
